《東華報》1913-01-25 刊載的〈瘋人逃脫〉。報載布里斯本地區一所院所的三名患者離院，巡警尋回其中一人。文章保留報紙對地名、族群與離院狀況的歷史描述，供比對院方與巡警的角色。

Feng ren tao tuo [Three Patients Leave an Asylum] appeared in Tung Wah Times (1913-01-25). The report describes three patients leaving a mental institution in the Brisbane area. A police officer found and returned one patient. The text documents the newspaper's portrayal of institutional supervision and police involvement.
